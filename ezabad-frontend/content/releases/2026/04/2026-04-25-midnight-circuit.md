## The Idea

This one started at 2 AM with a sample I chopped from an old keyboard recording.
The original loop was too bright, so I pitched it down a fourth and added some
tape saturation to knock out the highs. That gritty warmth became the spine of
the whole track.

## Key Production Choices

- **808 tuning** — matched exactly to the root note of the Rhodes loop (D minor)
  so the low end sits without clashing
- **Side-chain compression** — the pad pumps on every kick hit, creating that
  breathing, underwater feel
- **Reverb on the snare** — a short plate with a long pre-delay to keep the
  snap upfront while the tail washes back

## Mixing Notes

Kept the master chain light — just a gentle multiband comp and a limiter
hitting at -0.3 dBFS. The track breathes better without over-compression.
