## The Idea

Started from a jazz piano record I found. I chopped a two-bar phrase, pitched
it up a semitone, and layered it three times with slight timing offsets so it
sounds like a live take rather than a loop.

## Key Production Choices

- **Drum break** — layered a vintage MPC-style kick with a tight snare transient,
  then added a slightly delayed ghost snare for groove
- **Walking bass** — played live on a MIDI controller, not programmed, which
  keeps the natural swing intact
- **Vinyl EQ** — rolled off everything above 12 kHz to give the record that
  worn warmth without filtering the mid presence

## Mixing Notes

Intentionally left the mix a little rough. The saturation on the bus is doing
most of the glue work. No brickwall limiter — the track clips gently at the
loudest moments to simulate tape.
