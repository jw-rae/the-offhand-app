## The Concept

I wanted something that felt like it was accelerating the whole time — even
though the tempo is constant, the arrangement tricks your ear with rhythmic
displacement in the hi-hat pattern and a synth arp that offsets by half a beat.

## Key Production Choices

- **Synth arp** — a saw-wave lead through a fast attack/slow release envelope
  with a detune of 12 cents for width
- **Hi-hat roll** — 32nd-note triplet rolls on every third bar to build tension
- **Sub bass** — resampled from a sine wave, pitched to the root note, with
  light distortion to add harmonics that cut through on small speakers

## Mixing Notes

Heavy parallel compression on the drum bus — about 8 dB of gain reduction on
a 4:1 compressor blended in at 40%. This keeps the transients punchy while
filling out the bottom.
